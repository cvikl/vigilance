### What is the hackathon?
> What happens when you bring AI builders together with Google’s latest medical models, health experts, and the funders behind the next generation of health startups? We’re about to find out.
If you are a founder, engineer, clinician, researcher, healthcare professional or student, this is your opportunity to:
- Work at the frontier of possibility for AI in healthcare with advanced medical models
- Tackle real-world challenges set by health leaders & innovators
- Build solutions with Health AI Developer Foundations, which includes Google MedGemma and other open models.
- Collaborate with experts across AI, healthcare and industry
- Earn recognition from the organisations building the future of health
- Compete for a share of £13,000 in prizes

### The Challenge

---
Teams will tackle challenges across two tracks. In both tracks, your entry must utilise Health AI Developer Foundations models (which includes MedGemma). The themes for each problem track are below. Your team will receive detailed problem specifications, materials, judging criteria and technical setup guidelines in the run up to the event.
hxai-problem-tracks-notion.png

#### 1. Connected Care - From Treating Illness to Preventing It 
Challenge Set by Bupa
> Healthcare is evolving from treating illness to helping people stay healthy for longer. Bupa already offers a range of preventive healthcare services, from health assessments and genomic healthcare programmes to digital consultations, personalised health programmes and early mental health support. The opportunity isn't to create new preventive services, but to help people discover, understand and engage with the right services at the right time. Build an AI-powered solution that helps people engage with preventive healthcare through more personalised, timely and relevant experiences, empowering them to take action before they become unwell.

#### 2. Pathway Breakdown - Closing the Gaps at Care Handoffs
Challenge set by AlbionVC
> Patient pathways lose time and people at the handoffs (referral to diagnosis, diagnosis to treatment), but teams rarely have real-time visibility into where or why. Build something that surfaces this and helps a team act on it.

### Track 2 — AlbionVC, participant-facing task

> Build anything that gives a clinical team real-time visibility into where patients are being lost at pathway handoffs. The idea is yours — but it must satisfy every constraint below:
> - It must work from incomplete, inconsistent data without inventing what is missing
> - It must distinguish a stalled patient from one legitimately waiting
> - Every alert must name a specific next action and an owner
> - It must justify its prioritisation — why this patient, why now, ahead of the others
> - It must be usable inside an existing workflow, not as another system to check

### Judging

- Judges listen, score in Mentimeter, ask questions. **6–7 min per team: pitch + demo 4 min, questions 3 min.**
- Rubric (10 each): 1 Innovation & Effectiveness (real day-to-day pain; understood the user); 2 Constraint Compliance (all or some constraints + edge cases, **demonstrate live**); 3 Technical Quality (depth, architecture, use of the models — **only one slide allowed**); 4 Demo Completeness (working live demo, clear flows, **multiple test cases**); 5 Presentation Quality & Commercial Vision (narrative clarity, **credible commercial next step**, clarity of answers).
- Time keeping: on time 0; **−20 for every 2 minutes over.**
- Prize: one winner per track from the scores, plus one audience-choice award.

### Preparation guide

4 minutes covering: 1 team overview (what makes the team suited); 2 live demonstration; 3 technical architecture; 4 future roadmap. Any medium (slides, website, handwritten).
